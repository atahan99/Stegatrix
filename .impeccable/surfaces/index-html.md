# Surface: index.html (home + Create/Recover wizard)

Mode: Persuade (home hero) over Operate (wizard). Refinement of the incumbent neon world, pinned by the user.

Audience: portfolio reviewers with 30-60 s; repeat users of the wizard second.
Action: watch a message come out of an image, then start Create or Recover.
Proof: a live keyed-PNG embed of the demo image on load, no faked data.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page proves steganography by doing it in front of you: one image, three views (image, LSB plane, changes), and a real reveal. Refuses the neon-template habit of glowing every surface.
OWN-WORLD: Near-black violet ground; cyan = interface, magenta = hidden data only, green = success only, amber = warnings only. Glow reserved for hidden data, focus, active step and reveal. Panels are flat, hairline-bordered. Geist Bold for the wordmark and headings (user choice, 2026-10-20); monospace for data; system sans for prose.
STORY: Visitor sees an ordinary synthwave image, flips to LSB noise (same as any photo), flips to Changes and sees scattered magenta pixels, presses Reveal and reads the message. Then hides their own.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Wordmark and one-line explanation top-left; the image viewer dominates (about 60% width desktop, full width phone) with a three-way view switch under it; the reveal panel beside it (below on phone) with password prefilled and Reveal as the primary action; "Hide your own message" and "Recover a message" directly below.
FORM: Refined incumbent neon (user-pinned). Two degraded concept-seed runs (keys 627f0c20, then 085c12a8), neither adopted.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
